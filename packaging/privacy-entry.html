Zappel (Apple Watch) — Privacy

Zappel is an open-source arcade game for Apple Watch, in which a sprinkler turns caterpillars into butterflies. It runs on the watch alone and makes no network connection of any kind. This section is the canonical statement of what Zappel does and does not do with your data. The app is open source so that every claim here is independently verifiable. The repository is at github.com/excelano/zappel.

The short version

Zappel talks to nothing. There is no server behind it, no account to create, no advertising, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no third-party code. Its App Store privacy declaration is “Data Not Collected,” because the developer collects nothing and has nowhere to put it.

What stays on your watch

Zappel keeps your best score, the last wave you reached, and whether sound and haptics are turned on. It stores no names or initials. These live in the app's own preferences on the watch, are never sent anywhere, and are removed when the app is removed. Reset Best Score in Settings clears the score and the wave.

What leaves your watch

Nothing. Zappel makes no network request. It has no update check, no analytics service, no crash reporter and no remote logging, and it asks for no permission to reach your contacts, location, health data, microphone or anything else on the watch.

One thing outside the app's control

Apple collects its own figures about installs and, on devices where Share With App Developers is turned on, aggregate anonymous crash logs. Zappel contains no code that touches that data, but Apple may still show the developer aggregate, anonymised numbers. On the paired iPhone the control is at Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, and it applies to every app, not only this one.

How to verify the claims yourself

The full source is at github.com/excelano/zappel. Search it for anything that opens a network connection, such as URLSession, Network or a URL being fetched: there is none. Search for what it stores: every key it writes to its preferences is listed in one place, StorageKey in HighScoreStore.swift.
